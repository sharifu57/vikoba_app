# Meeting comments and late loan fines

Loan fines are stored in the existing `loan_installments.penalty_amount` column and added to `total_amount`. This is one payable charge per overdue installment, not a second independent entry in `fines`. Repayments reduce that combined balance; the loan cannot complete until principal, interest and fines are paid. The configured late fine is taken from the loan's application snapshot (group settings for older loans). A fine starts the day after the due date in Africa/Dar_es_Salaam, including partial payments. Fully paid, future and due-today installments receive no new fine. Write locks and the stored penalty prevent repeated assessments from charging again.

The daily scheduler includes ACTIVE and DEFAULTED loans. Opening a repayment schedule or recording a repayment also assesses overdue installments, so a missed scheduler run does not omit fines. Repayment recording and manual group assessment require LOAN_MANAGE. Mobile shows actual recorded fines; unpaid installment fines and OVERDUE statuses are red. These amounts are included in the schedule balance.

Meeting comments use `GET/POST /api/meetings/{meetingId}/comments`. The GET response includes `canComment`, `reason` and historical comments. The POST request accepts only `content`; author and group membership come from the authenticated user. Posting requires an active group membership, PRESENT attendance, a non-cancelled meeting and the meeting date to equal today's Tanzania date. LATE, ABSENT, EXCUSED and missing attendance do not qualify. Text must contain 1?2,000 characters. All active members of the group can read the retained history, with author and timestamp. Comments do not alter official meeting minutes.
